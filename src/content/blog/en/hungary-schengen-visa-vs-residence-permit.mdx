Many applicants call every status a visa, but Hungarian immigration planning starts by separating short stays from residence routes.

## Short-stay Schengen route

Tourism, family visits, business meetings, conferences, and similar short stays may fit the Schengen short-stay framework if the trip is within 90 days in a 180-day period.

## Residence permit route

Work, study, family reunification, business operation, remote work, investment, research, and other 90+ day purposes usually require a residence permit route.

## Why the distinction matters

Choosing a short-stay route for a long-term purpose can create refusal, overstay, or restart risk.

<Checklist heading="Before choosing a route">
- Confirm the intended stay length.

- Check whether work, study, family, or business activity is involved.
- Review current location and filing path before planning deadlines.

<Faq heading="Common questions">
Q: Can I use a Schengen C visa for long-term work in Hungary?
A: A short-stay visa is not a substitute for a work-related residence route. If the main purpose is employment, a residence permit route should be reviewed.
Q: Is every 90+ day stay a residence permit case?
A: In most practical planning, stays beyond the short-stay framework require reviewing a residence route, but the exact path depends on purpose and applicant facts.
