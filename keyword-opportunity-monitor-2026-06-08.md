## Executive signal

The strongest new opportunity this week is the June 2026 guest worker rule change. New guest worker residence permit applications are not possible from 6 June 2026 because no third countries are currently eligible for that permit category. Existing valid guest worker permits and already-paid pending applications have transitional protection. This creates urgent, high-conversion search intent for employers, workers, recruiters and agencies.
