"Hungary golden visa" is the common market phrase, but the official route is the guest investor visa and guest investor residence permit.

## Short answer

The guest investor route may fit a third-country national whose entry and stay is considered to be in Hungary's national economic interest due to a qualifying investment. Official materials list investment fund share and donation options.

- At least EUR 250,000 investment fund share issued by a qualifying real estate fund
- At least EUR 1,000,000 donation for eligible higher education, scientific, research, or artistic purposes
- Source of funds and compliance documents should be reviewed early
- Investor visa and residence permit timing must be planned carefully

## Visa and residence permit sequence

Some applicants may first need a guest investor visa and then apply for the residence permit in Hungary within the relevant deadline. Visa-free nationals and current permit holders can face different procedural questions, so timing should be checked before travel.

## Documents to prepare

Investor cases usually need passport, accommodation, insurance, investment evidence, banking records, source-of-funds evidence, tax or business records, and family documents where dependants are involved.

## Why manual review matters

Investor applications are high-value and document-sensitive. The route should be reviewed before selecting an investment product, transferring funds, or relying on family residence expectations.

## FAQ

### Is Hungary golden visa the official name?

No. It is a common market phrase. The official terminology is guest investor visa and residence permit for guest investors.

### Does EUR 250,000 automatically guarantee residence?

No. The investment route still depends on eligibility, documents, timing, authority review, and compliance checks.

### Can family members be included?

Family planning should be reviewed together with the investor route because family documents, sponsor status, and timing can affect the process.
