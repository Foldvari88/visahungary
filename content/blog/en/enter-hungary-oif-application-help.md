Enter Hungary is an electronic platform used in many Hungarian residence permit procedures, but it does not mean every applicant can file every case online from anywhere.

## Short answer

Enter Hungary is relevant for many residence permit submissions, extensions, employer-supported filings, notifications, and authority communications. The correct process still depends on route, current location, status, citizenship, and whether employer or host submission is allowed.

- Some cases start outside Hungary at a consulate
- Some cases can be submitted through Enter Hungary
- Employer or host entity submission may be available for specific categories
- Biometrics, appointments, and missing-document notices can still be part of the process

## Enter Hungary is not route selection

The platform is a filing channel, not a decision about which permit you need. Applicants should first identify the correct residence title, then check whether the case can be filed through Enter Hungary.

## Employer and host submissions

For several employment-related or host-supported routes, employer or host entity submission may be possible or required if the applicant provides written consent. HR teams should confirm the exact category before building the process.

## Missing documents and timing

A submission can still receive a missing-document or data notice. This can affect processing time, so documents should be reviewed before filing rather than uploaded in a hurry.

## FAQ

### Is OIF the same as NDGAP?

Applicants often still say OIF, while the current authority is commonly referred to in English as NDGAP. The practical issue is to follow the current authority process and forms.

### Can I apply from inside Hungary through Enter Hungary?

Sometimes, but not always. Being in Hungary and having an online account does not automatically make domestic filing available.

### Do I still need an appointment?

Some cases may involve biometrics, identity checks, card pickup, or regional directorate steps. The exact appointment need depends on the procedure.
