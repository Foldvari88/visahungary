Hungarian Card and EU Blue Card are both searched by qualified non-EU workers, but they are not interchangeable.

## Short answer

EU Blue Card is an EU-level highly qualified employment route with salary, contract, and qualification requirements. Hungarian Card is a Hungarian residence category for certain qualified work situations. The better route depends on the role, salary, qualification, nationality, employer, and long-term plans.

- Both routes need a real work purpose
- EU Blue Card is strongly tied to highly qualified employment requirements
- Hungarian Card eligibility depends on Hungarian route rules
- Employer or host submission through Enter Hungary may matter
- Family and mobility planning should be checked before choosing

## How to compare the routes

Start with the job offer, employment contract length, salary, qualification, and employer readiness. Then check whether the role and applicant facts fit EU Blue Card requirements or Hungarian Card conditions better.

## Salary and qualification questions

EU Blue Card cases commonly depend on salary threshold and qualification evidence. Hungarian Card cases may involve different qualification and route-specific requirements. Because thresholds and route rules can change, current official materials should be checked before filing.

## Employer process

Qualified work routes still need strong employer-side evidence. The application path may include Enter Hungary and employer or host entity involvement depending on category and consent.

## FAQ

### Is Hungarian Card the same as a Hungarian residence card?

No. "Hungarian Card" is a specific route name. It should not be confused with the generic idea of a residence card issued by Hungary.

### Is EU Blue Card only for tech workers?

No. It is for highly qualified employment where the legal requirements are met. Tech roles are common examples, but not the only possible roles.

### Which route is safer?

The safer route is the one that fits the documented job, salary, qualification, and employer facts. A route that sounds more prestigious is not safer if the evidence does not match.
