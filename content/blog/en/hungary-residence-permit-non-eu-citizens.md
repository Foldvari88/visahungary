If you are a non-EU citizen and want to stay in Hungary for more than 90 days, the key question is usually not "which visa" but which Hungarian residence permit route matches your purpose.

## Short answer

For a stay above 90 days, most third-country nationals need a purpose-specific Hungarian residence permit. Work, study, family reunification, remote work, business, investment, research, and long-term stay are assessed differently, so the safest first step is route selection before document preparation.

- Under 90 days usually points to Schengen short-stay rules
- More than 90 days usually points to a residence permit route
- The route depends on purpose, location, status, citizenship, and documents
- Prior refusal, overstay, missing documents, or urgent deadlines should be reviewed manually

## Main routes to compare

Employment may point to a residence permit for the purpose of employment, Hungarian Card, EU Blue Card, National Card, guest worker route, seasonal work, posting, or intra-corporate transfer. Study cases usually start with admission or active student status at a Hungarian institution.

Family cases depend on the sponsor's status and the relationship documents. Remote workers often ask about the White Card, but that route is built for foreign remote work and generally does not fit Hungarian employment.

## Documents usually needed

Most routes need passport, photo, accommodation proof, health insurance, financial means, and purpose evidence. Purpose documents are the part that changes most: work contracts, admission letters, family civil records, remote-work proof, company documents, or investment evidence may be needed.

## Where applications are usually submitted

The filing path may be consular, online through Enter Hungary, or handled with the immigration authority in Hungary depending on the route and facts. Being physically in Hungary does not automatically mean that the first application can be filed from inside Hungary.

## When manual review is recommended

Manual review is important if you have a prior refusal, overstay, expired status, unclear source of income, complex employer documents, uncertain family proof, or a deadline that is already close. These facts can change the route or the safest filing strategy.

## FAQ

### Is a D visa the same as a residence permit?

No. A D type visa is often connected to entry after a positive residence decision, while the residence permit is the long-stay status. The exact sequence depends on the case.

### Can I use a tourist visa for long-term work or study?

Usually no. Short-stay Schengen travel and long-term residence routes solve different problems. Work, study, family residence, remote work, and investment normally need a residence route.
