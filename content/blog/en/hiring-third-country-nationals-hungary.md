Hungarian employers hiring third-country nationals need more than a signed contract. The immigration route, employee status, employer documents, and submission path must fit together.

## Short answer

An employer should first identify the correct work-related residence route, then prepare job and company documents that support the purpose of stay. Depending on the route, an employer or host entity may handle parts of the process electronically through Enter Hungary with written consent.

- Confirm the employee is a third-country national
- Choose the work-related route before drafting the checklist
- Prepare contract, role, salary, workplace, and company documents
- Check whether the route allows employer-side Enter Hungary submission
- Review deadlines before the employee travels or changes status

## Employer route selection

The right route may be standard employment, EU Blue Card, Hungarian Card, National Card, guest worker, seasonal work, posting, or intra-corporate transfer. The route can depend on nationality, qualification, job type, salary, and whether the worker is already in Hungary.

## Documents employers usually prepare

Typical employer-side materials include a signed contract or preliminary agreement, job title, workplace, salary, working hours, company data, and any route-specific declarations or evidence. Some roles may also require qualification proof or regulated-profession documents.

## Enter Hungary and employer submission

Enter Hungary is often relevant for employment-related procedures. In some categories, employer or host entity submission is possible or required if the applicant gives the necessary written consent. This should be confirmed for the exact route before relying on it.

## Risk points for HR teams

Risk increases when the employee is already in Hungary with uncertain status, has a prior refusal, is changing employer, lacks accommodation, has a soon-expiring passport, or belongs to a category affected by current employment restrictions.

## FAQ

### What is a preliminary agreement?

It is commonly used to document the intended employment before the permit is issued. Its exact form and content should match the selected route and employer facts.

### Can HR submit everything for the employee?

Sometimes the employer or host entity can submit through Enter Hungary with written consent, but this is route-specific. The applicant may still need biometrics, documents, and authority steps.

### Should the company choose the route or the employee?

Both sides matter. The route must fit the job and employer, but also the employee's citizenship, location, qualification, and immigration history.
