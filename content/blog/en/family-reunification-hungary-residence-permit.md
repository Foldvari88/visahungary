Family reunification in Hungary depends on both sides of the case: the applicant's relationship documents and the sponsor's status in Hungary.

## Short answer

Family reunification may fit a third-country national joining a qualifying family member in Hungary, but the exact route depends on sponsor status, relationship type, housing, support, insurance, and current immigration position.

- Spouse and child cases are common, but documents must prove the relationship
- Sponsor status can change the route and rights
- Civil documents may need legalization, apostille, or translation
- Work rights should be checked before employment starts

## Sponsor status comes first

The sponsor may be a Hungarian citizen, third-country national residence permit holder, long-term resident, EEA citizen, or another qualifying person. The sponsor's route can affect whether family reunification is available and how the process is filed.

## Relationship documents

Marriage certificates, birth certificates, custody documents, divorce documents, and other civil records are often central. Foreign documents may need apostille, diplomatic legalization, or certified Hungarian translation depending on origin and use.

## Accommodation and support

Authorities usually need to see where the family will live and how the stay will be supported. Housing, funds, insurance, and sponsor documents should be checked before filing.

## FAQ

### Can my spouse work in Hungary on family reunification?

Work rights depend on the exact family route and notification or procedure requirements. Do not assume work can begin without checking the current rule.

### Can I join a student or White Card holder?

Possibly, but sponsor route restrictions matter. Family planning should be checked against the sponsor's exact residence title.

### What if my marriage certificate is from another country?

It may need apostille, legalization, or translation. This should be planned early because civil documents can take time.
