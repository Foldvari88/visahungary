Most Hungarian residence permit cases use the same document families, but the exact checklist changes by route.

## Short answer

Start with passport, photo, accommodation, financial means, health insurance, current status, and purpose evidence. Then add route-specific documents such as employer papers, admission letters, family records, remote-work proof, company documents, or investment evidence.

- Identity and passport documents
- Accommodation proof in Hungary
- Financial means or income evidence
- Health insurance or healthcare coverage
- Purpose-specific documents
- Apostille, legalization, or translation where needed

## Core documents

The core set usually includes a valid passport, biometric photo, application forms or online data, proof of lawful stay where relevant, Hungarian address documents, insurance, and financial evidence.

## Purpose documents

Purpose evidence is where cases differ. Work routes need employer support. Student routes need admission or student status. Family cases need relationship and sponsor documents. White Card cases need foreign remote-work proof. Investor cases need investment and source-of-funds records.

## Documents that take longest

Foreign civil records, diplomas, criminal records, company extracts, bank records, apostille, legalization, and certified translations can take longer than expected. Start these before deadlines become urgent.

## FAQ

### Is one checklist enough for every route?

No. A master checklist helps, but the final list should match the selected permit type and the applicant's facts.

### Do documents need Hungarian translation?

Some foreign documents may need certified Hungarian translation or other formalities. This depends on the document, country, and authority practice.

### Should I prepare accommodation before filing?

Usually yes. Housing is a common readiness point and should be consistent with the planned stay.
